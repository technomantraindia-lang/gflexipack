Inside the film

A pouch is three decisions, laminated together.

What keeps chips crisp for nine months isn't the print — it's the structure behind it. Scroll through the layers we bond for every job.

Print · BOPP / PET
Barrier · MET-PET / AL
Seal · LLDPE / CPP

One film. Your structure.

01

The print carrier

Your artwork is reverse-printed in up to eight colours on rotogravure — so the ink sits inside the laminate. It can't scuff, scratch or fade on shelf, no matter how rough the supply chain is.

02

The barrier core

Metallised PET or aluminium foil blocks oxygen, moisture and light. This layer is why roasted, fried and dried products stay fresh for months — and it's specified per product, not per catalogue.

03

The food-contact seal

LLDPE or CPP on the inside — food-grade, heat-sealable, and strong enough to run on high-speed filling lines without splitting a single seam.

04

Bonded into one film

Solvent-less lamination fuses the layers into a single web — then it's slit, pouched and batch-tested in our own QC lab before dispatch. Common structures we run:

Questions

Quality, answered.

Are your pouches food-safe?

Yes. We use food-grade inner seal layers (LLDPE or CPP) and food-safe lamination adhesives, and every batch is tested and documented before dispatch under our ISO 9001:2015 system.

What quality tests do you run?

Eight tests on every batch to ASTM standards — GSM, coefficient of friction (COF), bond strength, puncture resistance, burst strength, scrub resistance, heat-seal strength and tear strength — with full documentation at dispatch.

Do you offer recyclable packaging?

Yes. We produce 100% recyclable mono-material MDOPE/PE structures for EPR-compliant, sustainability-first brands, without sacrificing barrier or shelf appeal.

What certifications does G Flexipack hold?

We operate an ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management system across procurement, production and dispatch, and run ASTM-standard QC in an in-house laboratory.

Is quality documented for each order?

Yes. Raw material is inspected on arrival, colour is verified before print, the product is tested in-line at every stage, and a documentation pack accompanies dispatch.
