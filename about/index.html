About G Flexipack · Bharuch, Gujarat · Est. 2016

The pouches behind India's everyday brands.

G Flexipack was established in 2016 as a partnership firm in Bharuch, Gujarat, under the umbrella of Gangamani Industries and Gangamani Enterprise — group companies with 20+ years in manufacturing. That discipline carried straight into flexible packaging: we convert BOPP, polyester and PET/PE laminates into custom-printed pouches for the food, beverage, pharmaceutical and agrochemical brands that stock India's shelves, pharmacies and farms.

One roof, fourteen pouch formats, seven industries served — every pouch printed, laminated and converted in-house, start to finish.
