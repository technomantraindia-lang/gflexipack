Infrastructure

One factory floor, zero handoffs.

Most converters outsource at least one stage — printing at one vendor, lamination at another. We don't. Every roll that enters our Bharuch facility as plain film leaves it as a finished, inspected pouch, on the same floor, under the same quality system.

Rotogravure printing press.

9-colour rotogravure with automatic registration control — sharp detail and consistent shade-matching, run after run. Cylinders are engraved per job, so repeat orders reprint to the same shade months later, not just within a single run. Every printed roll is inline-inspected before it moves to lamination, catching registration drift or colour variance before it compounds downstream.

Colours9-colour
Max web width1,000mm
Print speedUp to 250 m/min
RegistrationAutomatic
SubstratesBOPP, PET, PE, metalised film

What this means for your order: the shade you approve on the first run is the shade you get on the fiftieth — no re-matching, no drift batch to batch.

Solvent-less & solvent-based lamination.

Food-safe multi-layer bonding — the barrier structure is matched to what's going inside the pouch, not a one-size-fits-all laminate. Solvent-less bonding is our standard for food-contact layers, avoiding residual solvent entirely, while solvent-based lamination is available where a structure calls for it — retort-grade adhesion or specific barrier films, for example.

Bonding typeSolvent-less & solvent-based
Max web width1,000mm
Line speedUp to 400 m/min
Structures2–3 ply laminates

What this means for your order: solvent-less as standard keeps residual solvent off food-contact layers, with solvent-based available for structures that need it.

Precision slitting lines.

Tension-controlled winding to exact reel widths — output either goes straight to pouching or ships as finished film rolls. Consistent tension across the reel prevents telescoping and wrinkling in transit, so what leaves our slitting line arrives at yours ready to run, not needing to be re-wound first.

Max unwind width1,250mm
Slit tolerance±0.5mm
SpeedUp to 500 m/min
WindingTension-controlled

What this means for your order: tight tolerance means every reel feeds your pouching line cleanly — no jams, no wasted film from misaligned edges.

Pouching & bag-making lines.

Stand-up, flat-bottom, 3-side seal, centre-seal, zipper and spouted formats — fourteen pouch styles, converted in-house. Seal strength and closure fitment are checked continuously through the run, not spot-checked at the end, so a pack that fails a seal test gets caught before it reaches your case count, not after it's already boxed.

Formats14 pouch styles
Fill weight range5g – 25kg
OutputUp to 100 pouches/min
ClosuresHeat seal, zipper, spout

What this means for your order: from a 5g sachet to a 25kg industrial sack, it's the same in-house line and the same QC checklist.
